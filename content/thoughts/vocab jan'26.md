**pavlovian** - something that is automatic or triggered by a cue. the term came from Ivan Pavlov's experiments showing how animals associate neutral stimulus (a bell ring) to an activity or reaction (consume food). learned about this from [jzhao's post](https://jzhao.xyz/posts/2025)
